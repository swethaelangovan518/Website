Editorial Policy

Peer-review process for ICSAI 2026 manuscript submissions

Submission

Review Process Overview

1
Plagiarism Check
Initial Plagiarism Screening

To uphold scientific integrity, one of our editors will run Turnitin on each new submission to check for potential plagiarism. Papers that do not pass the plagiarism check will be desk-rejected immediately.

2
Scope Check
Initial Editorial Check

The Publication Chairs conduct an initial check to ensure the submission falls within the scope of the conference and decide whether it merits further review. Once passed, the manuscript is assigned to reviewers for double-blind peer review.

3
Peer Review
Expert Independent Review

Each selected submission is reviewed by at least two or three independent expert reviewers on originality, validity, quality, academic merit, and readability.

4
Evaluation
Editorial Judgement

The peer-review reports from the experts are evaluated by one of the editors against international scientific standards to determine their validity and relevance.

5
Author Feedback
Review Reports Sent to Authors

Logical and valid peer-review reports are sent to the authors for revision. For reports deemed invalid, the editor may assign a new reviewer or make a judgement based on their own expertise.

6
Author Response
Manuscript Revision

Authors are required to respond to all peer-review comments in detail and revise their paper according to every point raised by the reviewers.

7
Editor Review
Revised Version Evaluation

The revised manuscript is evaluated by the editor to determine whether all points raised by the reviewers have been fully and satisfactorily addressed.

8
Re-Review
Reviewer Re-Evaluation

The editor returns the revised manuscript to the reviewers for a second evaluation of the changes made and their adequacy.

9
Final Decision
Editor-in-Chief Acceptance

If the reviewers approve the revised manuscript, the Editor-in-Chief makes a final decision on acceptance for publication in the conference proceedings.
