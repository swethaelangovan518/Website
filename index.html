Overview

About GAISNCT

The International Conference on Generative AI, Intelligent Systems, and Next-Generation Communication Technologies (GAISNCT-2026) is a premier international platform dedicated to advancing research, innovation, and collaboration in the rapidly evolving domains of Artificial Intelligence, Intelligent Computing, and Communication Technologies.

The unprecedented growth of Generative Artificial Intelligence — including Large Language Models (LLMs), multimodal AI systems, and autonomous intelligent agents — has revolutionized human-technology interaction. Simultaneously, advancements in next-generation communication networks such as 5G, 6G, IoT, edge computing, and cyber-physical systems are enabling seamless connectivity across diverse applications.

GAISNCT-2026 encourages the convergence of Computer Science and Electronics & Communication Engineering disciplines to foster transformative technologies supporting sustainable development and digital transformation. The conference will feature keynote addresses, technical paper presentations, invited talks, industry sessions, workshops, and panel discussions.
