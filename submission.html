Peer Review

Article Acceptance Requirements

All submitted papers are peer-reviewed by two or three expert reviewers from the program committee. The following criteria must be met for acceptance:

  1. The article must be original writing that enhances the existing body of knowledge. Review articles and surveys are acceptable even if new data are not presented.
  2. Results reported must not have been submitted or published elsewhere prior to submission.
  3. Experiments, statistics, and other analyses must be performed to a high technical standard and described in sufficient detail for reproducibility.
  4. Conclusions must be presented in an appropriate fashion and be fully supported by the data presented.
  5. The article must be written in clear, intelligible Standard English throughout.
  6. Appropriate references to related prior published works must be cited correctly.

At the Conference

Presentation Instructions

🎙 Oral Presentation

  • Maximum 15 minutes total, including Q&A.
  • Use USB drive or CD; scan for viruses beforehand.
  • Meet your session chair 10 minutes before the session.
  • Copy slides (PPT or PDF) to the session laptop in advance.
  • Email a backup copy of slides to yourself.
  • Each room has: LCD projector, screen, pointer, microphone, and laptop with PowerPoint & Adobe Reader.
  • If using your own laptop, test the connection before your session.
  • Embedded movies must be well formatted and linked to the main file.

🖼 Poster Presentation

  • Maximum poster size: 59.4 × 84.1 cm (A1).
  • Posters must be condensed and visually attractive.
  • All text and figures must be legible from 1 metre away.
  • Authors must stand by their poster throughout the session.
  • Be prepared to discuss and answer questions from delegates.
